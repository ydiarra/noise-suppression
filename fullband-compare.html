Blind test (headphones)

Pick a situation (quiet room, typing, people talking behind you, fan, sudden noise), talk, and switch between A, B and C. For each letter, rate voice naturalness and noise removal from 1 to 5 and note artefacts (metallic voice, clipped words, pumping). Then click "Reveal", copy the notes and send them. "New order" reshuffles the letters for the next situation.
